# Burrow Brawl characters: the Squirmlings (v1, 2026-11-11)

Lucas asked (2026-11-11) for the playable characters to be worms instead of moles. They replace
the Molekins everywhere. Worms as a creature are fine, but the design is our own. **Do not**
make a pink cartoon worm with big round eyes, a smooth tube body and a bulbous head. That look
belongs to another game.

## Concept
**Squirmlings** are chunky toy worms built from stacked rings, like a wooden stacking toy or a
string of clay beads.

- **Silhouette:** 5 rounded ring segments that taper from head to tail, with visible creases
  between them. The head is the widest ring and is slightly flattened, like a gumdrop. The body
  stands in an S-curve and bobs ring by ring when it moves (each ring lags the one in front).
- **Colors:** the rings alternate between the team color and a cream or soft-earth band, so
  every team reads clearly at a distance. Matte clay finish with fingerprint texture. Never a
  solid pink.
- **Face:** small, wide-set black bead eyes with a white glint, low on the head. A stitched
  zig-zag or button-thread mouth. Fuzzy felt "eyebrow" tufts carry the emotion. No large
  cartoon eyeballs.
- **Extras:** a tiny tuft of felt on the tail tip that flicks when the Squirmling is idle. Hats
  sit on the head ring (all 30+ hat ideas from content.md carry over). Accessories: goggles,
  scarf, bandana, backpack-sized tool belt around the 2nd ring.
- **Movement:** an inchworm-style scrunch and stretch for walking, a coil-and-spring jump, and
  a backflip that rolls through the rings. Hurt: the rings squash and wobble. Knocked out: the
  rings pop apart and roll, leaving a team-colored grave marker (style is customizable).
- **Tone:** goofy and squeaky. The voice packs from content.md stay.

## Name use
- The creature: Squirmling (plural Squirmlings).
- The game title stays **Burrow Brawl**, which fits worms well.
- In code and data: `squirmling`, replacing `molekin`. The legacy three.js client can be renamed
  in passing; new Godot work uses the new name from the start.
